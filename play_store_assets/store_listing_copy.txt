1. APP TITLE (Max 30 characters):
UniRemote - Universal TV Remote

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION (Max 80 characters):
Universal TV Remote for Smart TV (Wi-Fi) and Non-Smart TV (Infrared IR Blaster).

3. FULL DESCRIPTION (Max 4000 characters):
UniRemote is a modern, fast, and reliable universal TV remote control app that lets you control your television directly from your Android phone. 

Whether your TV is a modern Smart TV or a classic TV with an infrared sensor, UniRemote provides seamless control using both Wi-Fi and built-in Infrared (IR Blaster) technology.

★ KEY FEATURES:
• Dual-Mode Control: Supports both Wi-Fi Smart TV control and Infrared (IR Blaster) hardware transmission.
• Smart TV Auto-Scan: Automatically discovers Smart TVs connected to your local Wi-Fi network.
• Android TV Remote Protocol: Direct TLS-encrypted connection with fast key response for Android TVs (Toshiba, Hisense, Sony, TCL, Mi, etc.).
• Pre-Configured Multi-Brand IR Library:
  - Toshiba TV (CT-8560 Smart / CT-90325 Regza profiles)
  - Samsung TV
  - LG TV
  - Sony TV (SIRC 40 kHz protocol)
  - Mi / Xiaomi TV
  - TCL / Thomson TV
  - Panasonic TV
  - Roku TV (Wi-Fi ECP protocol)
• Tactile Remote Experience: Skeuomorphic remote design with haptic click vibration feedback.
• Complete Control: Power ON/OFF, D-Pad navigation, Volume Rocker, Channel Rocker, Input/AV Switch, Mute, Home, Back, and 0-9 Keypad.
• Wake-on-LAN (WoL): Turn on supported sleeping TVs over local Wi-Fi.

★ HOW TO USE:
1. Wi-Fi Smart TV Mode:
   - Ensure your phone and Smart TV are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
   - Tap "Auto Scan" or select your TV.
   - For Android TVs, enter the 6-character code shown on your TV screen once to pair.
2. Infrared (IR) Mode:
   - Requires an Android phone with a built-in IR blaster.
   - Select your TV brand and point the phone towards the TV.
