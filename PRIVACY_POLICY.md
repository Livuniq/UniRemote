# Privacy Policy for UniRemote

**Last updated:** October 20, 2026

Livuniq ("we", "our", or "us") built the **UniRemote - Universal TV Remote** application as a free / utility service. This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone who decides to use our application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**UniRemote does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal credentials (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use UniRemote.
- **No Personal Data Stored:** We do not track your location, browsing habits, media consumption, or identity.

---

### 2. Device Permissions and How They Are Used
UniRemote only requests permissions that are technically required to control televisions:

- **Local Network / Wi-Fi Access (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `INTERNET`):**  
  Used solely to discover and communicate with Smart TVs connected to the same local area network (LAN) using standard protocols (such as mDNS/SSDP and Android TV Remote v2 TLS pairing). No internet browsing history or personal data is collected or sent outside your home network.
- **Infrared Transmitter (`TRANSMIT_IR`):**  
  Used exclusively to transmit standard consumer infrared pulse signals to control non-smart TVs via your phone's built-in IR blaster hardware.
- **Vibration Feedback (`VIBRATE`):**  
  Used to provide tactile haptic feedback when remote buttons are pressed.
- **Wake Lock (`WAKE_LOCK`):**  
  Used temporarily to keep the connection alive while sending commands to the TV.

---

### 3. Data Safety & Third-Party Sharing
- We do **NOT** sell, trade, or share any user information with third parties.
- We do **NOT** use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or data brokers.
- All pairing codes and TV connection parameters are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to any external server.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
